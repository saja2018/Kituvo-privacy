Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Kituvo ("Kituvo", "we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Kituvo mobile application.

Kituvo does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the application's main features.

In the current version of Kituvo, we do not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties.

Some information, such as calculation history or application settings, may be stored locally on your device so that the application can provide its features correctly.

This information is stored locally and is not intentionally transmitted by Kituvo to our servers.

4. Internet Access

The current version of Kituvo does not require an online account to use its main calculation and conversion features.

5. Advertising

The current version of Kituvo does not contain third-party advertising or Google AdMob.

If advertising or other third-party services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of Kituvo does not intentionally share personal information with third-party services.

7. Children's Privacy

Kituvo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to use its main features.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its services change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Kituvo, you can contact us using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.
